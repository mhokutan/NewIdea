PromoVote is a short vertical video community for a specific group: independent game and app creators, small online sellers, and the scouts who enjoy finding them early.

The app's centre is a prediction and reputation game that the App Store does not offer. A scout watches a full screen video and calls either "Will blow up" or "Not for me." The call locks and resolves seven days later against the real result, building a public Scout Score and a public record of calling things early. Duel shows two videos at once and asks the scout to pick the one that will blow up first. Creators get a social profile with followers and real audience feedback before they spend money on advertising.

The app opens directly into this loop: Today's Drop, a full screen vertical video feed with the call buttons, is the first and default screen on every device size, with the scout's own score and streak shown at the top.

On iOS, no promo card carries a download, get, or install style button. A creator's own link lives one tap deeper, inside that creator's profile, the way a link in bio works on other social apps. Explore leads with creators and hashtags; a secondary segment on that same screen holds a chart ranked by scouts' call accuracy (not by downloads or clicks) and a set of content category filters, used to browse creators and promos already in the app, not third party listings.

Every promo in this build belongs to the developer's own products: our own game, our own apps, our own shop. No third party app is offered, sold, or ranked. The app never fetches or displays any App Store data: no prices, no star ratings, no screenshot galleries, no App Store metadata of any kind. Ranking is never for sale, Team picks are never paid, and the Scout Score is a reputation score with no cash value. The app is free and has no in app purchases in this version.

Report and Block are under the "..." button on every promo and creator page. Account deletion: Profile, "..." at the top right, Delete account. Review account: Profile, Sign in, Continue with email, review@promovote.com, then the code from the sign in information field. Creators are separate accounts and cannot vote; user video upload is not open to the public in this version.
