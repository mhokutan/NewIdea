Description:
```
PromoVote is a prediction game for people who spot hits early.

Every day Today's Drop gives you 7 short promo videos from creators. Watch, then make your call: Will blow up, or Not for me. Seven days later the crowd decides. Right calls grow your Scout Score, and a weekly streak keeps you coming back.

What you can do:
- Make a call on every promo in Today's Drop
- See your results, your accuracy and your Scout Score
- Keep a weekly streak
- Play the Duel: two promos side by side, pick the one that blows up first
- Follow creators, save promos and share them with friends

Fair by design: paying never buys a spot on the charts, Team picks are never paid, and your Scout Score is a reputation score with no cash value.

For creators: a profile page with your logo, banner and links, free stats for every promo, and gifts you can offer to scouts.
```

App Review notes:
```
PromoVote is a short video community built around one game: a scout watches a promo, calls whether it will blow up, and seven days later the call is scored by the crowd into a Scout Score (reputation only, no cash value). The first screen is Today's Drop: one video at a time with the call bar (Not for me / Will blow up) and a strip at the top with the scout's Score, streak and right calls (a brand new scout sees "Make your call. Results in 7 days."). Duel is its own tab. Results are under Profile.

In this build every promo (23 promos from 3 creators) belongs to the developer's own studios and is labeled "Made by the PromoVote founder". No third party app is offered. Store links are not on the videos; a creator's links live on the creator's profile page, like a link in bio. Creators are separate accounts and cannot vote. User video upload is not open.

Report and Block are under the "..." button on every promo and creator page. Account deletion: Profile, "..." at the top right, Delete account. Review account: Profile, Sign in, Continue with email, review@promovote.com, then the code from the sign in information field. The app is free; there are no purchases in this version.
```
