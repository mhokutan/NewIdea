# Poleris: Manifest & Rituals

A calm daily practice for manifestation: write an intention, then a two-minute ritual each day (read your affirmation, give thanks, see it), a 21-day journey, breathing and guided meditation.

- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6792844860
- Google Play: not live yet (version 2.0 in review, package `com.miapera.poleris`)

All footage is real app screens (web build) with a sample user, no real personal data. No prices, no "free trial" text. Vertical 1080x1920, H.264, 30 fps, silent, closing card with app icon and "Poleris".
