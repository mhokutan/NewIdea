# Ad Review Checklist

Every ad is reviewed by a human before going live. Not legal advice.

## 1. Advertiser
* Account verified (email + payment card name match). New accounts get extra care.
* Has rights confirmation checkbox: owns or has permission for all content, trademarks, music, images.

## 2. Category
* **Allowed (phase 1):** indie games. Later: apps, YouTube channels, low risk DTC.
* **Always prohibited:** illegal products, scams, phishing, malware, counterfeit, adult/sexual content, weapons, drugs, deceptive finance, copyright infringement, hate, violence.
* **Closed for now (restricted):** alcohol, gambling, crypto, financial products, health/medical, dating, political ads.

## 3. Video
* Length 10 to 45 seconds.
* No misleading claims, no fake UI (fake close buttons, fake notifications).
* Music and footage rights look plausible (no copyrighted songs, no other brand's logo used as if official).
* Game trailers: content fits 18+ audience; flag extreme gore or sexual content.

## 4. Link (CTA)
* Destination matches the ad (same game/product).
* URL scanned (Google Safe Browsing or similar). No redirects chains, no download of executables.
* Re-check links periodically after approval (cloaking).

## 5. Offer (optional key / code / beta)
* Offer is never tied to a positive rating, vote or comment (FTC 16 CFR 465, Steam rules).
* Distribution is first come first served, no lottery (sweepstakes law).
* Keys come from the advertiser's own account, no cash value, not transferable.

## 6. Decision
* **Approve**, **Reject** (with policy reason), or **Request Changes** (what exactly).
* Advertiser can appeal once. Advertisers can never delete negative user comments, only report them.
