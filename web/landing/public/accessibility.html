Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 2, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use PromoVote, including people with disabilities.

Our goal

Our goal is for promovote.com to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. We design with keyboard navigation, readable text, sufficient color contrast and screen reader support in mind, and we will keep improving as we build the product.

Known limitations

  • The waitlist form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block bots. This check is provided by a third party, and in rare cases it may be hard to complete with some assistive technologies.
  • We have not yet completed a formal accessibility audit.

Report a problem

If you have trouble using any part of the site, or you cannot join the waitlist, email hello@promovote.com with the subject line "Accessibility". Please tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology you use. We will try to reply within 5 business days and, if needed, help you join the waitlist another way.
