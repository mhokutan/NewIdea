This short summary is for everyone, and it is also our Notice at Collection for California residents.

  • Your rights: see Your rights below, or email hello@promovote.com.

1. Who we are

PromoVote is operated by MIA PERA TRANSPORTATION LLC (doing business as PromoVote), a limited liability company organized in the State of New Jersey, United States ("PromoVote", "we", "us"). Our mailing address is available on request at hello@promovote.com. For the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

Contact: hello@promovote.com

2. What we collect

Information you give us when you join the waitlist:

  • Your email address.
  • Your role: creator or streamer, game developer, brand or business, or viewer.
  • An optional link to your channel, game, product or website (not asked from viewers).

Information we record automatically with your waitlist entry:

  • Your country, based on the request metadata that our hosting provider Cloudflare attaches to each request. We only store the country, not your city or precise location.
  • The date and time you joined.

Information processed for delivery and security, but not stored with your entry:

  • When you visit the site, your browser sends technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for. Cloudflare uses this to deliver the website and protect it from attacks.
  • When you submit the form, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a real person. This check uses technical signals such as your IP address and browser information. We do not save your IP address with your waitlist entry.
  • We use Cloudflare's privacy-friendly analytics, which does not use cookies and gives us aggregate numbers such as page views and visitor countries. It does not build a profile of you.

We do not have user accounts or take payments on this website yet. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or social media tracking tools.

3. How and why we use it

We use your information only for the purposes below. If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, we also list the legal basis we rely on under the GDPR or UK GDPR.

  • To add you to the waitlist and invite you when your wave opens. Legal basis: taking steps at your request before a possible agreement (Article 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in running an orderly early access program (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To email you about early access and PromoVote updates. Legal basis: your consent, which you give by joining the waitlist (Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in our emails or by emailing us. Withdrawing does not affect emails we already sent.
  • To understand who is interested (for example, how many creators or viewers joined, and from which countries) so we can plan the launch and open access in the right order. Legal basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To keep the site and the form safe, block bots, spam and abuse, and fix technical problems. Legal basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To comply with the law, respond to valid legal requests, and protect our rights. Legal basis: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) and legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have balanced our interests against your rights and keep the data we use to a minimum. You can object at any time (see Your rights).

We do not use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising on other websites.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising (also called targeted advertising). We share it only in these cases:

  • Service providers (processors). Today we use one service provider: Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare provides our hosting and content delivery network, the database where the waitlist is stored (Cloudflare D1), the Turnstile bot check, email routing for our @promovote.com addresses, and cookieless analytics. Cloudflare processes data on our behalf under its data processing terms. You can read Cloudflare's privacy policy and the Turnstile privacy addendum.
  • Email. Emails you send to hello@promovote.com or support@promovote.com are forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to a mailbox used by our team at a third-party email provider, which stores those messages under its own terms.
  • Legal reasons. If required by law, court order or a valid government request, or if needed to protect the rights, property or safety of PromoVote, our users or others.
  • Business transfers. If PromoVote is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that deal. We will tell you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

If we add new service providers (for example, an email sending tool), we will update this policy.

5. International transfers

PromoVote is based in the United States. Cloudflare operates a global network, so your information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where Cloudflare operates. These countries may have different data protection laws than your country.

When we transfer personal information from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland to other countries, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK addendum) included in our provider's data processing terms, or an adequacy decision such as the EU to US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

  • Waitlist data (email, role, link, country, sign up time): until PromoVote launches plus 24 months, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the conversation, and no longer than the waitlist period above unless the law requires more.
  • Technical and security data processed by Cloudflare is kept for short periods under Cloudflare's own retention rules.

If you ask us to delete your data, we may keep a minimal record (for example, your email on a suppression list) so that we do not email you again, and only where the law allows it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. For example: the site is served only over HTTPS, the waitlist database is not public, admin access is password protected, we do not store IP addresses with waitlist entries, and only people who need the data can access it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a data breach that affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires. To report a security problem, see our Security page.

8. Your rights

You can make any of the requests below by emailing hello@promovote.com. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights. We may need to confirm your identity, usually by asking you to reply from the email address on the waitlist. We aim to respond within 30 days, and always within the time the law requires (for example, one month under the GDPR, which can be extended in some cases, and 45 days under California law).

EEA and UK residents (GDPR and UK GDPR). You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information and get a copy of it.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your information (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict how we use your information in certain cases.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and object at any time to direct marketing.
  • Data portability: receive your information in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). We would appreciate the chance to fix your concern first, so please contact us.

Everyone else. Wherever you live, you can ask us to see, correct or delete your waitlist information, and you can unsubscribe from our emails at any time. We will honor these requests.

9. California privacy rights (CCPA and CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, gives you the rights below.

Categories of personal information we collected in the past 12 months:

  • Identifiers: email address. Source: you. Purpose: waitlist and communications.
  • Professional or commercial information: your role and an optional link to your channel, game, product or website. Source: you. Purpose: waitlist planning and early access.
  • General location: country only (not precise geolocation). Source: Cloudflare request metadata. Purpose: launch planning.
  • Internet or network activity: technical data used by Cloudflare for delivery, security and the bot check, plus aggregate cookieless analytics. Source: your browser. Purpose: security and site operation.

We disclose these categories only to our service provider (Cloudflare) for the business purposes described above. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by California law.

Your rights:

  • Right to know and access: what personal information we collected about you, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of recipients, and a copy of the specific pieces of information.
  • Right to delete the personal information we collected from you, with limited legal exceptions.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of today. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first and provide a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect it.
  • Right to non-discrimination for using any of these rights.

Global Privacy Control. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. If your browser sends GPC, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of sale and sharing for that browser. Because we do not sell or share personal information, this does not change how the site works for you today.

Authorized agents. You can use an authorized agent to make a request for you. We may ask for proof that you gave the agent permission and ask you to confirm your identity directly with us.

Do Not Track. Because we do not track you across websites, the site works the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

10. Other US state privacy laws

Residents of other US states with consumer privacy laws (for example Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and others) may have rights to confirm whether we process their personal information, access it, correct it, delete it, get a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale of personal information and certain profiling. We do not sell personal information, do not use it for targeted advertising, and do not use it for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We honor these requests for all US residents, whether or not a specific state law applies to us.

Appeals. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision or emailing hello@promovote.com with the subject line "Privacy appeal". We will respond within the time required by your state's law. If you are not satisfied with the result, you can contact your state Attorney General.

11. Children

PromoVote is only for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 joined the waitlist, email us and we will delete their information.

12. The planned PromoVote product

We will publish an updated privacy policy and tell waitlist members by email before that happens. Points and Scout Score in the planned product will have no cash value.
