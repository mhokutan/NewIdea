Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026. Effective: October 7, 2026.

Draft: this policy is being reviewed by a lawyer before the public launch. If anything here is unclear, email support@promovote.com.

PromoVote is a place to watch short promos from games, apps, shops and creators, make a call on which ones will blow up, save them and follow creators. This policy explains, in plain language, what personal information we collect in the PromoVote apps for iPhone and Android, the PromoVote web app, and the promovote.com website (including the waitlist), why we collect it, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

The short version: we collect what the app needs to work and to stay safe. We do not show ads from ad networks, we do not use third party analytics or tracking tools, we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal information.

Notice at Collection (summary)

This short summary is for everyone, and it is also our Notice at Collection for California residents.

  • What we collect in the app: your email address and name from Sign in with Apple or Google, your birth year (we ask your full birth date to confirm you are 18 or older, then keep only the year), your country, your app language, your account type (Scout or Creator), your public profile (handle, display name, bio, photo, banner, links and category), your calls, saves, follows, blocks, reports and gift claims, and simple activity counts (which promos you watched for at least 3 seconds, button and link taps), plus the IP address and device type recorded with your sign in session.
  • What we collect on the website and waitlist: your email address, the role you pick, an optional link, the country your request came from and the date you joined, plus cookieless visit counts.
  • Why: to run your account and the app (feed, Today's Drop, calls and Scout Score, saves, follows, gifts, creator stats), to keep PromoVote safe (age check, reports, blocks, spam and fraud prevention), to run the waitlist, and to meet legal duties.
  • How long we keep it: account data until you delete your account, then 30 more days in case you change your mind, then we delete it. Viewing counts are deleted after 400 days. Waitlist data until PromoVote launches plus 24 months. Details are in section 6.
  • Selling and sharing: we do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by California law, and we do not use precise location.
  • Your rights: you can delete your account inside the app at any time. See Your rights below, or email support@promovote.com.

2. What we collect

When you watch as a guest (no account):

  • You can watch every promo without signing in. To count views fairly, the app creates a random device id on your phone or browser. It is not linked to your name, email, advertising id or any other app. With it we record which promos were watched for at least 3 seconds (one count per promo per day), whether a promo was watched to the end, which buttons were tapped, and the country of the request.

When you create an account:

  • From Sign in with Apple or Google: your email address and your name, as Apple or Google share them with us, an account id from Apple or Google, and the sign in tokens they give us. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only get the relay address Apple creates. Google may also share a link to your Google profile picture. We never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Email code sign in: today this is only open to the App Review test account. If we open it to everyone, we will process your email address to send you a one time code.
  • Age check: we ask your full date of birth to confirm that you are 18 or older. We keep only the birth year and the time you confirmed your age.
  • Account details: your account type (Scout or Creator, chosen at sign up; one email is one account), your country (from your choice or from the country of your request, never your precise location), your app language, and the date you accepted our Terms and Community Guidelines and which version you accepted.
  • Your public profile: handle, display name, bio, profile photo and banner (if you add them), and for creators also links, category and a main button.
  • Sign in session: while you are signed in, our sign in system stores a session with the IP address and device type (user agent) of the device that signed in. We use this to keep you signed in and to stop account abuse.

When you use the app with an account:

  • Calls: your "Will blow up" or "Not for me" call on each promo, when you made it, and its result. From these we calculate your Scout Score, level, accuracy and weekly streak.
  • Saves, follows and blocks: the promos you save, the creators you follow, and the creators you block.
  • Reports: what you reported, the reason, and any details you add.
  • Gifts: which creator gifts you claimed and when. Gift codes are stored encrypted.
  • Activity counts: the same daily view and tap counts described for guests, linked to your profile instead of a device id, and taps on a creator's links.
  • Creators: the gifts you set up (title, code, description, link, end date, stock) and the promos, videos and statistics on your page.
  • Messages you send us: if you email support, we get your email address and what you write.

Stored only on your device, not sent to us:

  • The optional daily "Today's Drop is ready" reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone. We do not run a push server and do not receive a push token.
  • Small app settings, such as which results you have already seen in the "You called it" reveal, your sign in token (kept in the phone's secure storage) and the random guest device id.

On the promovote.com website and waitlist:

  • If you join the waitlist: your email address, the role you pick (creator or streamer, game developer, brand or business, or viewer), an optional link to your channel, game, product or website, your country based on Cloudflare request metadata, and the date and time you joined.
  • When you submit the form, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a real person using technical signals such as your IP address and browser information. We do not save your IP address with your waitlist entry.
  • We count website visits with our own privacy-friendly counter that runs on Cloudflare. It does not use cookies. For each page view we store the page, the site you came from, your country, your device type (mobile or desktop) and a random daily code made from your IP address and browser. That code changes every day, so it cannot follow you across days, and we do not store your IP address.
  • When you visit, your browser sends technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for. Cloudflare uses this to deliver the website and the app and to protect them from attacks.

We do not collect your contacts, photos library, microphone, precise location, advertising id, payment card details or postal address. If you add a profile photo, the app only uploads the single image you pick.

3. How and why we use it

We use your information only for the purposes below. If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, we also list the legal basis we rely on under the GDPR or UK GDPR.

  • To provide your account and the app: sign in, your profile, the feed and Today's Drop, calls and their results, Scout Score and streaks, saves, follows, gifts, and creator pages and stats. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you, the Terms of Use (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To confirm you are 18 or older and to apply the age rules in our Terms. Legal basis: contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interests in keeping minors off an 18+ service (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To keep PromoVote safe and fair: review reports, act on abuse, enforce blocks, stop spam, fake accounts and vote manipulation, and protect sign in. Legal basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), and legal obligation where the law requires us to act (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • To count views and taps so creators see honest statistics and so charts and Scout Score results reflect real activity. Guest views are counted but never decide rankings. Legal basis: contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To show the app in your language and region. Legal basis: contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To answer your messages and support requests. Legal basis: contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To run the waitlist and email waitlist members about early access and PromoVote updates. Legal basis: steps at your request (Article 6(1)(b)), legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), and for update emails your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To improve PromoVote using aggregate numbers (for example, how many promos were watched each day). Legal basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To comply with the law, respond to valid legal requests, and protect our rights. Legal basis: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) and legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have balanced our interests against your rights and keep the data we use to a minimum. You can object at any time (see Your rights).

Scout Score and call results are calculated automatically from your calls and other people's activity. They are a game of reputation with no cash value and no legal or similarly significant effect on you. We do not use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects. If we remove content or an account, a person makes or reviews that decision.

4. What others can see, sharing and service providers

What other users can see. Your handle, display name, bio, profile photo and banner are public. For creators, your links, category, promos, follower count and active gift are public. Other users do not see your email, birth year, country, the promos you saved or watched, the people you blocked, or your reports. A creator you report or block is not told who you are. Creators see aggregate numbers about their own promos (for example views, saves, new followers, link taps and how many people claimed a gift), not which person did what. Results of calls appear in aggregate (for example the share of Scouts who called a promo).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising (also called targeted advertising). We share it only in these cases:

  • Service providers (processors).
    • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the app's server and the website, runs our databases (Cloudflare D1), will store uploaded images and videos (Cloudflare R2) when uploads open, delivers content, provides the Turnstile bot check and routes email sent to our @promovote.com addresses. Cloudflare processes data on our behalf under its data processing terms. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and the Turnstile privacy addendum.
    • Email. Emails you send to hello@promovote.com or support@promovote.com are forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to a mailbox used by our team at a third-party email provider, which stores those messages under its own terms.
  • Apple and Google. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, Apple or Google confirm your identity to us under their own privacy policies. Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) also distribute the app and receive information about your download under their own policies. We build the app with Expo (650 Industries) build tools; Expo does not receive personal information about you from the app.
  • Links you open. When you tap a creator's link or a gift link, you leave PromoVote and the other site's privacy policy applies. For links to websites (not app stores), we may add simple campaign tags (utm_source=promovote and similar) so the creator knows the visit came from PromoVote. These tags do not contain any information about you.
  • Legal reasons. If required by law, court order or a valid government request, or if needed to protect the rights, property or safety of PromoVote, our users or others.
  • Business transfers. If PromoVote is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that deal. We will tell you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Our service providers must protect your information at least as well as this policy does and may only use it to provide their service to us. If we add new service providers, we will update this policy first.

When we transfer personal information from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland to other countries, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK addendum) included in our provider's data processing terms, or an adequacy decision such as the EU to US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

6. How long we keep it and how to delete it

Delete your account in the app: tap Profile, tap the ... button at the top right, tap Delete account and confirm. You are signed out on every device right away and your profile is hidden. If you cannot use the app, email support@promovote.com from the email address of your account. Full steps are on our Delete account page.

  • Account data (email, name, Apple or Google link, birth year, country, language, profile, links, calls, Scout Score, saves, follows, blocks, gift claims, and for creators their promos, gifts and statistics): kept while your account is open. After you ask to delete it, we keep it for a 30 day grace period so you can restore the account by signing in again. After 30 days it is permanently deleted.
  • Sign in sessions (including IP address and device type): deleted when you sign out or delete your account, and expired sessions are cleaned up every day.
  • Daily view and tap counts: deleted after 400 days. After an account is deleted, any remaining counts only contain an internal id that no longer points to a person.
  • Reports you made: kept without your name after your account is deleted, when we need them to keep the platform safe.
  • Deletion records: we keep a short record that an account was deleted (an internal id and the dates), so we can prove we did it.
  • Records we must keep by law, for example about a legal request, for as long as the law requires.
  • Waitlist data (email, role, link, country, sign up time): until PromoVote launches plus 24 months, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the conversation.
  • Technical and security data processed by Cloudflare is kept for short periods under Cloudflare's own retention rules.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. Data stored only on your device (the reminder, local settings, the guest device id) is removed when you delete the app. You can turn the daily reminder off at any time in the app settings or in your phone's notification settings.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. For example: all traffic uses HTTPS, the databases are not public, sign in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage, gift codes are encrypted, we keep only your birth year, admin access is restricted, and only people who need the data can access it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a data breach that affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires. To report a security problem, see our Security page.

8. Your rights

You can delete your account inside the app at any time. For any request below, email support@promovote.com. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights. We may need to confirm your identity, usually by asking you to write from the email address on your account. We aim to respond within 30 days, and always within the time the law requires (for example, one month under the GDPR, which can be extended in some cases, and 45 days under California law).

EEA and UK residents (GDPR and UK GDPR). You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information and get a copy of it.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete (you can edit most of your profile in the app).
  • Delete your information (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict how we use your information in certain cases.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and object at any time to direct marketing.
  • Data portability: receive your information in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). We would appreciate the chance to fix your concern first, so please contact us.

Everyone else. Wherever you live, you can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, and you can unsubscribe from our emails at any time. We will honor these requests.

9. California privacy rights (CCPA and CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, gives you the rights below.

Categories of personal information we collected in the past 12 months:

  • Identifiers: email address, name, account ids from Apple or Google, your PromoVote handle, IP address recorded with sign in sessions, and a random guest device id. Source: you, Apple or Google, and your device. Purpose: accounts, sign in, security and view counting.
  • Characteristics: birth year, used only to confirm you are 18 or older. Source: you. Purpose: age check.
  • Professional or commercial information: for waitlist members, your role and an optional link; for creators, your public profile, links, promos and gifts; for everyone, gifts you claimed. Source: you. Purpose: providing the service and early access.
  • Internet or other electronic network activity: calls, saves, follows, blocks, reports, promo views and taps, link taps, device type, and cookieless website visit counts. Source: you and your device. Purpose: providing the app, creator statistics, Scout Score, safety and site operation.
  • General location: country only (not precise geolocation). Source: you or Cloudflare request metadata. Purpose: language, regional content and statistics.
  • Inferences: your Scout Score, level, accuracy and streak, calculated from your calls. Source: our calculation. Purpose: the Scout Score game.
  • User content: your profile text and images, reports and messages to support. Source: you. Purpose: providing the service and safety.

We disclose these categories only to our service providers for the business purposes described above, and display your public profile to other users as described in section 4. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by California law.

Your rights:

  • Right to know and access: what personal information we collected about you, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of recipients, and a copy of the specific pieces of information.
  • Right to delete the personal information we collected from you, with limited legal exceptions.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of today. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first and provide a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect it.
  • Right to non-discrimination for using any of these rights.

Global Privacy Control. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. If your browser sends GPC, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of sale and sharing for that browser. Because we do not sell or share personal information, this does not change how PromoVote works for you today.

Authorized agents. You can use an authorized agent to make a request for you. We may ask for proof that you gave the agent permission and ask you to confirm your identity directly with us.

Do Not Track. Because we do not track you across websites or apps, PromoVote works the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

10. Other US state privacy laws

Residents of other US states with consumer privacy laws (for example Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and others) may have rights to confirm whether we process their personal information, access it, correct it, delete it, get a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale of personal information and certain profiling. We do not sell personal information, do not use it for targeted advertising, and do not use it for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We honor these requests for all US residents, whether or not a specific state law applies to us.

Appeals. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision or emailing support@promovote.com with the subject line "Privacy appeal". We will respond within the time required by your state's law. If you are not satisfied with the result, you can contact your state Attorney General.

11. Children

PromoVote is only for people 18 and older. We ask for your date of birth before you can create an account and do not let anyone under 18 sign up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it. If you believe someone under 18 is using PromoVote, report the profile in the app or email support@promovote.com.

12. Purchases, ads and tracking

  • No purchases today. The current version of PromoVote is free and has no in-app purchases. Paid extras for creators (such as Boost, the Trailer Test report and the PromoVote Pro plan) may come in a later version. When they do, payments will be handled only by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), who act as the seller and send your receipt. We will receive a confirmation of what was bought, not your card details, billing address or other payment data. We will update this policy before we launch any purchase.
  • No ad networks. Promos in PromoVote are posted by the creators and businesses that own them. We do not use advertising networks or advertising SDKs, and we do not use your data to target ads. If paid Boost placements launch, they will be labeled "Sponsored" and will not use your personal data for targeting beyond language and country.
  • No third party analytics or tracking. The app does not include third party analytics, attribution or crash reporting SDKs. We do not use your device's advertising id, and we do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. That is why the app does not ask for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • Scout Score and gifts. Points and Scout Score have no cash value. Claiming a creator's gift never depends on your calls, follows or watch time, and we do not give your email to the creator when you claim a gift.
