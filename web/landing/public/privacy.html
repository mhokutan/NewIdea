Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

PromoVote is currently in early access. This page explains what we collect on this website and why.

What we collect

  • Waitlist details you give us: your email address, whether you are a creator, developer, brand or viewer, and an optional link to your channel, game or website.
  • Country: the country your request came from, based on standard network information. We do not store your IP address with your waitlist entry.
  • Bot protection: we use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that sign ups come from real people. Cloudflare processes limited technical data for this check under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To contact you about early access to PromoVote and product updates.
  • To understand who is interested so we can open access in the right order.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising on other websites.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Cloudflare, our hosting provider, in the United States and other locations where Cloudflare operates.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@promovote.com. Every email we send will include a way to unsubscribe.

Age

PromoVote is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

When PromoVote launches we will publish a full privacy policy. We will tell waitlist members about important changes by email.
