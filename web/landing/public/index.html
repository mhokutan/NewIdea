FAQ

Questions

Is PromoVote free?

For viewers, always. For creators, posting your promo and having a profile will be free. Paid options like detailed Trailer Test reports come later, and early waitlist creators get their first report free.

Who can post a promo?

New YouTubers, streamers on Twitch and Kick, TikTok and Instagram creators, indie game developers, app makers and small brands. Every promo is reviewed before it goes live.

Do viewers get paid to watch?

No. PromoVote is not a "watch ads for cash" app. Points and Scout Score have no cash value. People are here to discover new things, and creators can share perks like beta keys with the community.

What is not allowed?

Scams, malware, adult content, weapons, drugs, gambling, political ads and anything that breaks the law or someone else's copyright. You also can't ask for votes in exchange for rewards.

When does it launch?

We are opening in waves, starting with the web and then iOS and Android. Join the waitlist and you'll hear from us first.
