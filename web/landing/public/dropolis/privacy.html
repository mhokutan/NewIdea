Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026. Draft: this text is being reviewed by a lawyer before the public launch.

Dropolis is a game where you build a city and then a colony on Mars. This policy explains in plain language what the game collects and why.

What we collect

  • A guest account. When you open the game we create an account for you with a random id and a device id from your phone, so your progress can be saved. No name or email is needed.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google (optional). If you choose to sign in, we receive an account id from Apple or Google and, if they share it, your email address. We use it only to keep your progress across phones.
  • Profile. The player name and avatar you choose, and your country (taken from your connection, you can change it).
  • Game data. One cloud save of your game, and your scores for the leaderboards (city level, deliveries, Mars colonists).
  • Gameplay events. Things like when a session starts and ends, tutorial steps, finished missions, level ups and your first rocket. We use them to understand where players get stuck and to make the game better.
  • Online signal. While the game is open it tells our server every few minutes that you are playing, so we can count players online. We keep only the last time we saw you.

What is public

Leaderboards show your player name, avatar, country and score to other players. Do not use your real name as your player name if you do not want it shown.

Ads

  • Dropolis shows rewarded video ads only when you tap "Watch an ad". There are no banner ads and no forced ads.
  • Ads come from Unity LevelPlay, with Unity Ads and ironSource Ads, provided by Unity Technologies. See the Unity privacy policy.
  • To show and measure ads, our ad partners receive the advertising ID of your phone (the Android advertising ID, or the Apple IDFA only if you allow tracking on iOS), information about your device and the app, and how you interact with ads.
  • Ads are personalised only if you agree. The game asks for your choice from mission 6, and you can change it any time in Settings > Privacy choices. On iOS the system tracking prompt also applies.
  • If you do not agree, the game works the same and the ads you see are not personalised.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data. On Android, only if you agree in the consent window, our ad partner (Unity Ads through ironSource LevelPlay) receives your device's advertising ID so it can show rewarded ads. See Ads above.
  • We do not use third party analytics tools: game events go only to our own server. The only third party that can receive device data is our ad partner, as described in Ads, and only with your consent. The iPhone version shows no ads at the moment and does not track you.
  • The game shows a rewarded ad only when you choose to watch one. You can change or withdraw your ad consent at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.

Where it is stored and for how long

Your data is stored with Cloudflare, our hosting provider, in the United States. We keep it until you delete your account. When you delete your account, your account, save, scores and gameplay events are deleted right away.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time, see Delete account. You can ask us for a copy of your data or ask a question by emailing support@promovote.com.

Children

Dropolis is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.
