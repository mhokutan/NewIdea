Delete your account

You can delete your Dropolis account and all its data at any time.

In the game

Open the game settings and tap Delete account, then confirm. Your account, cloud save, leaderboard scores and gameplay events are deleted right away. This cannot be undone.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, email support@promovote.com from the email of your Apple or Google account, or tell us your player name, and write "Delete my Dropolis account". We will delete it and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign in links (Apple or Google)
  • Your cloud save
  • Your scores on every leaderboard
  • Your gameplay events
