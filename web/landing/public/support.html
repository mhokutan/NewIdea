Support

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Need help with the PromoVote app? Email support@promovote.com. We answer within 2 business days. Please tell us your username and your phone (iPhone or Android).

Common questions

What is a call?

On every promo you can call Will blow up or Not for me. Calls resolve after 7 days. Right calls add points to your Scout Score. The Scout Score is a reputation score with no cash value. A wrong call never takes points away.

Why does Today's Drop show 7 promos?

Today's Drop is a small daily pick, so it is easy to finish. After it, tap Keep watching or open the New tab to see more.

How do I sign in?

Use Sign in with Apple or Google on the Profile tab. You can watch without signing in.

How do I report or block?

Tap the ... button on any promo or creator page, then choose Report or Block. You can also email us. See our Community Guidelines.

How do I delete my account?

Profile, the ... button at the top right, Delete account. Full steps: Delete your account.

I am a creator. How do I get on PromoVote?

Choose a creator account when you sign up, or email hello@promovote.com.
