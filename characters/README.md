## Rules for every character (never break them)
1. Openly AI. Profile name ends with "(AI)" or the bio starts with "AI character". Every post has the platform AI label on
   (TikTok "AI-generated content", Instagram "AI info", YouTube "altered or synthetic content") and the caption ends with #AIcharacter.
   On PromoVote the promos carry "Made with AI" and the page shows "AI character".
2. Made by the PromoVote founder: the bio says it, the same way the three founder studios do.
3. No made up life experience and no fake testimonial. The character never says "this app changed my life", "I lost 10 kg",
   "I made money". It shows the product doing what it really does ("Day 3 of the 21 day plan: box breathing, 4 counts each").
4. Never presented as a real person, never chats with followers as if human, never asks for money, gifts, DMs or private photos.
5. Clean: no sexual poses, no revealing outfits, no suggestive captions. Adult (18+ look, clearly over 25), never childlike.
6. Original faces only. Never resemble a real celebrity, influencer or streamer. No brand logos except our own apps.
7. Following, liking or downloading is never rewarded ("follow for a prize" is banned on every platform).
8. Store links: App Store link only for iPhone viewers, Google Play only for Android (in PromoVote this is automatic).
