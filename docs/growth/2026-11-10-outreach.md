# Outreach and Hauling Empire card (2026-11-10)

From the outside analysis (docs/review/2026-11-10-fable-analysis.md), suggestions 1 and 2.
Rules: never pay viewers, never tie a gift or reward to an install, a vote or a review. The founder sends every
message from his own account (no automated DMs).

## 1. Indie developer outreach (goal: 30 messages, 10 yes)

Where to find them: X (#indiedev, #screenshotsaturday, #gamedev with a trailer in the post), r/IndieDev and
r/indiegames trailer posts, itch.io new releases, Steam "Upcoming" with a trailer. Pick small teams with a 10 to 60 s
trailer posted in the last 30 days. Keep a simple list: name, link, date sent, answer.

### First message (X DM, Reddit chat or email)
```
Hi {name}, I saw the trailer for {game} and really liked {one real detail}.

I run PromoVote, a new app where players watch short game trailers and call which ones will blow up. It's free and live on the App Store and Google Play.

Can I put your trailer in it for free? After 2 weeks I'll send you a short report: how many people watched it, how long they watched, and how many called it "will blow up". We are early and the audience is small, so I won't promise big numbers, but the feedback is real.

I'd only use the trailer you already posted, with your OK. No cost, no catch, and you can ask me to remove it any time.

{your name}, PromoVote
```

### Follow up (only once, 4 days later, if no answer)
```
Hi {name}, just a quick follow up on the free trailer slot for {game}. If it's not a fit, no problem at all. Thanks!
```

### When they say yes
```
Great, thank you! Please reply with:
1. The trailer file or link (10 to 60 seconds; we show up to 30 s for now),
2. Your store or Steam link,
3. A one line description.
I'll send you the link when it's live, and the report in 2 weeks.
```
Then: save their written OK (screenshot of the message), send the files to Claude, and Claude adds the trailer to
the founder managed account flow until creator uploads open.
